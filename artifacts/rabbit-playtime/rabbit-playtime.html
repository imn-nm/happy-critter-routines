Try it

  • Home: tap the rabbit and it turns to wave at you. Tap the floor and it hops over.
  • Dress up: seven accessories in three slots. Tap the rabbit to twirl and see the outfit from the side.
  • Bath time: rub the sponge over the mud, rinse under the shower, then towel it fluffy.
  • Carrot catch: drag, tap, or use ← → to catch carrots for 30 seconds. Golden ones count three.

How it fits the app

  • Opens during free time. It unlocks in the windows the schedule frees up, including time left over when a task is finished early.
  • No stars here. Stars only come from parents, so play earns carrots for fun and a personal best, nothing to spend.
  • Never a sad pet. Missed carrots bounce away, mud is just a reason for a bath, and every round ends with a cheer.
  • Faces you only when playing with you. It stays in its 3/4 view while it hops, catches and bathes, and turns to face the child to wave, get dressed and cheer.
  • The outfit sticks. Whatever it's wearing carries into every game, and is remembered on this device.
