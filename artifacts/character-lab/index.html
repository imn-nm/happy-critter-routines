MyToki Dress-Up Lab

Try Biscuit and her friends in every colour, outfit piece, prop and room. Everything is drawn by the app's own pixel code, cell for cell, on the round MyToki screen.

Outfit pieces and props are placed for Biscuit and moved to fit this character, so a piece may sit a pixel off.
